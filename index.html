The NodeIP Technical Pipeline

In-house vector indexing and multi-agent AI architecture ensuring speed, low cost, and data sovereignty.

Stage 1

Bulk Ingestion

Automated batch extraction of USPTO Open Data Portal JSON, EPO, and WIPO datasets. Structural chunking of claims and IPC/CPC codes.

Stage 2

Local Vector DB

768/1024-dim dense embeddings stored in self-hosted Qdrant with HNSW indexing for instant sub-30ms similarity lookups.

Stage 3

Multi-Agent Refinement

Agentic co-pilot probes adjacent classification clusters, discovers white spaces, and hardens claims against prior art overlaps.

Stage 4

Automated Synthesis

Multi-pass generative drafting engine creating full patent claims (independent/dependent) or publication-ready research reports.
